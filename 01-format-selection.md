# 01 · Format selection

## Which format for which job

| Format | Best for | Funnel stage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thought leader ads** | Trust and credibility | Top | Promotes a person's post (e.g. a founder). Reads as a voice, not an ad. |
| **Single video** | Reach and education | Top to middle | Explain the problem or show the product. Captions on. |
| **Single image** | Clear offers and retargeting | Middle to bottom | One message, one CTA. Fast to test. |
| **Document ads** | Lead magnets (guides, playbooks, reports) | Middle | Preview a few pages in-feed; gate the rest with a lead form. |
| **Sponsored content** (boosted posts) | Amplifying posts that already perform organically | Any | Let organic performance pick the winners, then put spend behind them. |

## A simple full-funnel sequence

```
Thought leader / video      →   builds trust with the ICP
        ↓ (retarget engagers and video viewers)
Document ad (lead magnet)   →   captures leads with a form
        ↓ (retarget openers who didn't convert)
Single image (offer / call) →   books meetings
```

## Format notes

### Thought leader ads
- Choose posts that already earned organic engagement.
- Keep the voice personal: a story, an opinion, a lesson. No brand-speak.
- Use them to warm audiences, not to ask for a meeting.

### Document ads
- The first pages do the selling: put the most useful insight up front.
- Gate after enough value to earn the form fill.
- Title the document like a result ("The 5-step playbook to…"), not a topic.

### Single video
- Hook in the first few seconds; most people won't watch to the end.
- Burn in captions.
- Keep it short; one idea per video.

### Single image
- Headline states the benefit; image supports it.
- Strong for retargeting and offers (demo, call, trial).

## Copy rules

- **Intro text:** lead with the hook; the rest gets truncated behind "see more".
- **Speak to one role.** "For CTOs at scaling startups" beats "for businesses".
- **One CTA per ad.**
