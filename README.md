# LinkedIn Ads Playbook

**Every B2B format, shipped: document, single image, single video, thought leader and sponsored content.**

LinkedIn is the most expensive click in B2B, and the most precise. This playbook is about spending it on the right people with the right format.

---

## Principles

- **Format follows the job.** Trust, reach, lead capture and offers need different formats.
- **Targeting starts with the ICP.** Account and contact lists beat broad job-title guesses.
- **Earn attention before the ask.** Thought leader and video build trust; document ads and offers convert it.
- **Quality over volume.** LinkedIn leads are expensive, so judge on cost per qualified lead and meetings.
